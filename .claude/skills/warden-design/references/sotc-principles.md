# What Shadow of the Colossus does (principles to learn, pairings to avoid)

Source: IGN's per-colossus walkthrough pages (read 2026-11-21), paraphrased.

## Principles worth borrowing

1. **The way in is the puzzle.** Each colossus is mounted differently: make it kneel by wounding a leg,
   make it lift a foot and shoot the sole, bait a weapon swing onto a pad that breaks its armour, hide so it
   stoops, wait for it to surface, lure it over a geyser, blind it so it rams a wall, deflate what keeps it
   aloft, ride a falling pillar's bridge.
2. **The warden's own force builds the route.** Its strikes break armour, floors, columns and bridges into
   ramps and ledges the player then uses.
3. **Every mark changes the situation.** A mark closes and moves; breaking one makes it roll, dive, drop a
   weapon, lower its head, crumble the platform, or plunge to the ground and throw you off.
4. **Marks sit on different body parts** (tail, wing tips, back spines, belly flaps, palm, shoulder), so the
   body is traversed like a level, often with leaps between holds.
5. **The player can steer or herd it** (strike to steer from the head, drive it back with fire, keep it
   following with arrows).
6. **Skill changes the reward** (a higher fall = a longer stun; a quick return to the next mark = fewer cycles).
7. **Safe spots on the body** (a plate on the hip, the shoulders, the back of the neck) let the player rest
   and plan between shakes.
8. **Escalating set pieces** — the arena itself is consumed by the fight (pillars felled one by one, a
   bridge smashed, a tower crumbled).

## Off-limits pairings (too recognisable — do not reproduce)

- Kneel-by-stabbing-the-leg-fur giant with a crown mark.
- Four-legged beast felled by shooting its lifted soles.
- Armoured swordsman whose swing breaks armour on a stone disc; sword-as-ramp.
- Horse-like colossus outwitted from burrows / mounted by the tail, neck stab to lower the head.
- Bird mounted from water platforms with marks on tail and wing tips.
- Bearded giant that stoops to look under an overhang.
- Electric eel with lit back spines, dive-and-resurface ride.
- Wall-climbing lizard shot off the wall to expose its belly.
- Crab/tortoise flipped by geysers.
- Sand worm blinded by arrows so it rams a wall.
- Bull driven off a cliff with fire to crack its shell.
- Water beast steered by striking its teeth into towers.
- Flying serpent brought down by bursting its gas sacs.
- Charging bull toppling the pillars the player stands on.
- Sword giant whose stomps/swings carve a path upward; final mark in the palm.
- Towering final giant approached through a trench under fire, hand-to-hand transfers.
