# Creature design — the two wardens

Both wardens are living animals, not statues. They share only the game's rules (fur = climbable, two sigils,
no health bar). Everything else — silhouette, anatomy, palette, surface, habits — is separate.
The reference sheets are rendered from the in-game models (front / side / rear / three-quarter / head /
surface / scale / back), with labels; no image generator was available, so there is no painted concept art.

## 1. The Warden — "the Mourner"  (`src/game/colossus.js`, sheet: `warden-mourner.jpg`)
Concepts weighed: a stone ruin-giant (rejected: a golem), a walking tree (rejected: no anatomy to climb),
a long-armed old ape-giant grown over by its own years (chosen: clear holds, heavy readable motion).

- Build: upright, ape-shouldered; arms longer and heavier than the short bowed legs; ~27 m tall.
- Hide: thick umber leather in deep folds, liver-dark blotches, pale belly and palms, basin dust to the knees.
- Bone wherever the body met the world: face mask, tusks, knee / knuckle calluses, elbow spurs, claws.
- Coat: old-gold locks on the back, hips, legs, forearms, crown (the only holds). Moss on the shoulders.
- Eyes: two small amber lights sunk behind the mask; they blink.
- Signature features: (1) mask of bone over the face; (2) left horn broken to a hollow stump, right horn whole;
  (3) healed scar from right shoulder to left hip; (4) three bone spurs through the right shoulder;
  (5) moss mantle over a gold coat.
- Habits: ribs fill and empty; jaw hangs on the breath out and gapes when hurt or thrashing; weight rolls
  onto the planted leg; when untroubled it lifts its face and lows (the "mourning").

## 2. The Strider — "the Tarn Runner"  (`src/game/strider.js`, sheet: `strider-tarn-runner.jpg`)
Concepts weighed: a stone ram (rejected: statue), a giant elk (rejected: an enlarged animal), a scaled
deer-like runner of the high water with horns laid back as reins (chosen: fits "broken like a wild horse").

- Build: long-legged quadruped, deep chest, tucked loin, arched neck, whip tail.
- Hide: fine slate-teal scales, dark saddle along the spine, pale barring on flank and leg, cream belly.
- Ivory: a row of scutes either side of the mane, one great scute over the croup (breaks off), brow and blaze.
- Coat: ash-silver mane on breast, withers, back and neck (the only holds), beard and tail brush. Legs bare.
- Eyes: narrow ice-blue; they blink. They turn warm when it is enraged.
- Signature features: (1) two ribbed horns swept back over the neck, left tip chipped; (2) ivory blaze from
  poll to nostril; (3) dewlap and beard; (4) the scute row; (5) barred legs with cloven hooves.
- Habits: flanks breathe; jaw works while it drinks and gapes when it bucks; a quick toss of the horns.

## How they differ at a glance
| | Mourner | Tarn Runner |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | upright biped, heavy arms | long horizontal quadruped |
| Skin | warm umber wrinkled leather | cold teal scales with barring |
| Hard parts | bone mask, tusks, spurs | ivory scutes, blaze, swept horns |
| Coat | old gold | ash silver |
| Eyes | amber | ice blue |
| Temper | slow, grieving, crushing | shy, quick, bolting |
